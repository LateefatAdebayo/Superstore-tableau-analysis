# Superstore Performance Dashboard – Lateefat Adebayo

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard built to explore sales performance across the Superstore dataset. The goal was to better understand how the business is performing in terms of revenue, profitability, and regional trends, and to highlight key patterns that could support decision-making.

The dashboard focuses on simplifying complex data into clear, visual insights that can be easily interpreted by both technical and non-technical users.

## What the Dashboard Shows
The dashboard is designed to answer a few key business questions:
•⁠  ⁠How are total sales performing over time?
•⁠  ⁠Is the business growing or declining?
•⁠  ⁠Which product categories are driving profit?
•⁠  ⁠Which regions are performing the best and which need improvement?

To answer these, the dashboard includes:
•⁠  ⁠KPI summaries (Total Sales, Sales Growth Rate, Profit Margin, Average Order Value)
•⁠  ⁠A time-series view of sales and profit trends
•⁠  ⁠A breakdown of profit by product category
•⁠  ⁠A comparison of sales performance across regions

## Key Insights
From the analysis, a few patterns stood out:
•⁠  ⁠Sales show an overall upward trend with a noticeable peak around November, likely influenced by seasonal demand and promotions.
•⁠  ⁠Technology is the most profitable category, followed by Office Supplies, while Furniture contributes significantly less to profit.
•⁠  ⁠The West and East regions outperform the Central and South regions in terms of total sales.
•⁠  ⁠The business is experiencing positive growth, as reflected in the sales growth rate KPI.
